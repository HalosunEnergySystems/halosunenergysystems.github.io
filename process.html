How a project runs

Six steps from roof to running meter

  1. 1

    Site Survey

    An engineer visits your roof or land to check shading, structure and available load.

  2. 2

    System Design

    We size the plant to your actual consumption and roof, and prepare drawings and layout.

  3. 3

    Approvals & Subsidy Filing

    We handle net-metering paperwork and government subsidy applications on your behalf.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Qualified crews install panels, inverter and wiring — most rooftop jobs finish in three to seven days.

  5. 5

    Commissioning

    Utility inspection, net meter installation, and formal handover of your plant.

  6. 6

    Monitoring & Maintenance

    Ongoing performance tracking and scheduled service to protect your generation over time.
