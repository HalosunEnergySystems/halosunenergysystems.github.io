Why we do a load audit on-site

  • Motor-driven appliances (AC, fridge, water pump) draw 3-5x their running watts for a moment on startup - undersizing here is the most common battery-backup mistake, and it needs real inverter-rating knowledge, not a web form.
  • How deep a battery can safely be discharged differs by chemistry (lithium vs. tubular) and materially changes usable capacity.
  • Charging and discharging a battery both lose some energy, and the loss depends on the inverter-battery pairing.
  • Your solar array also needs to be able to recharge that battery day-to-day, not just store it once.
